BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION 15 Aquaculture is vital for global food security as the world population is projected to reach 9.8 billion by 2050. Natural resources are at their limit and the ocean alone may not suffice to meet the demand for food. Aquaculture is an efficient solution for food production, yet its environmental impact needs to be assessed. It is estimated that by 2050, aquaculture will produce one-third of all seafood. Sustainable aquaculture 20 practices are being adopted to ensure the production of healthy and safe food while preserving the environment. Water quality monitoring (WQM) is an important aspect of aquaculture, as it ensures the health and safety of aquatic organisms. It is essential to track water properties such 25 as temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen (DO), salinity and turbidity to detect changes in water quality. WQM is traditionally done through the use of physical sensors, which are expensive and require frequent maintenance. Thus, there is a need for an alternative and cost-effective sensing system for WQM. 2

The system comprises one or more nodal units with two or more sensor nodes, a central node, and a water medium.
