Introduction

Virtual sensing

  • VS techniques, also called soft sensing, proxy sensing, inferential sensing, or surrogate sensing, are used to provide feasible and economical alternatives to costly or impractical physical measurement instrument.

  • This technique predicts parameters using mathematical models, which are not measured using physical sensors.

  • A VS system uses information available from other measurements and process parameters to calculate an estimate of the quantity of interest.

  • In this work, expensive DO sensor is replaced with solubility equations.

  • Solubility equations used to estimate the DO:

    • The Weiss Equations

    • The Benson and Krause Equations

    • The Garcia and Gordon Equations

Node S

  • Implemented virtual sensing in Node S of WQM system.

  • Here costly DO sensors are replaced by virtual sensing.

  • Virtual sensing uses solubility equations and interdependency equations.
