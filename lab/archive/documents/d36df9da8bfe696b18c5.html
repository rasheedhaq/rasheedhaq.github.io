BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION Aquaculture is considered as an integral component of global food security as the world population is expected to reach 9.8 billion by 2050. The harvest from natural 15 resources is mostly beyond their biological limits, and the ocean alone may not meet the demand for food required for this growing population. Aquaculture is one of the efficient solutions for food production but, it has impact on the environment. Aquaculture practices may involve inefficient energy utilization and wastage of water 20 resources. To minimize the impact on the environment and to optimize the use of resources, Water Quality Monitoring [WQM] plays an important role in aquaculture. Usually, the farmer assesses the farm by continuous manual monitoring, which will not provide all the required information. This traditional method is tedious, time- 25 consuming, and incapable of meeting the requirements. They can take actions only when the farmer has observed a visible change in the condition of the fish or environment. By this time, environmental factors have already changed dramatically. The procedure to bring the farming environment back to normal is expensive once the damage is already happened. 2
