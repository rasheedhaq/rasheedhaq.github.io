01Problem

Useful aquaculture monitoring needs more than a prediction model: it needs a practical way to collect measurements while managing sensing cost and energy use.

02Approach

At NIT Puducherry, I developed a low-cost IoT water-quality monitoring system as part of a DST-funded project. The broader research combines monitoring, virtual sensing and deep learning, with studies of measurement scheduling and solar energy harvesting.

03Results

A recorded demonstration documents the prototype. The thesis brings together the sensing system and modelling studies; the related research led to three Indian patents granted to NIT Puducherry.
