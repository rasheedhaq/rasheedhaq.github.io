Learning what the water does next.

Comparing recurrent and hybrid models for water-quality time series.

01Problem

Monitoring tells us the current state of a pond. Prediction asks what comes next, across parameters with different behaviour and modelling requirements.

02Approach

The research compares recurrent architectures and hybrid convolutional-recurrent models. The companion implementation separates data preparation, model definitions, training, evaluation and hyperparameter experiments such as window size, batch size and learning rate.

03Results

The work was published in IEEE Access in 2022. The thesis comparisons show that no standalone model dominates every parameter and dataset, motivating hybrid approaches rather than a single universal winner.
